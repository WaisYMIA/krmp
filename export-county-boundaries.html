Export county boundaries

A one-time job that saves Kenya's 47 official county boundaries as a small file on your own website, so the ROC page map loads instantly and no longer depends on an outside server.

  1. Open this page on a computer with internet and press Start (it can take a minute on a slow connection).
  2. Check the preview looks like Kenya, then press Download.
  3. Put the downloaded file in your website folder at data/kenya-counties.geojson (next to roc.html).
Ready. Press Start.
