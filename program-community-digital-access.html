KRMP Community Digital Access

Ensuring no community is left behind.

Why it matters

Digital access shouldn't depend on where you live. Every Rural Opportunity Centre provides assisted digital access for people who need it — from a farmer checking market prices to a student submitting an application.

Who it serves: Students, farmers, entrepreneurs, small businesses and community organisations.
